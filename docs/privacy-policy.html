Top 10 Deals – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Who we are

This policy covers the Top 10 Deals app for Android (package com.top10.products), which shows the ten best weekly offers at grocery stores in Belgium. The app is made by Insomnia dev, an individual developer based in Belgium, who is the data controller for this app.

Contact for anything about privacy: insomniatft@gmail.com.

Top 10 Deals is not affiliated with Delhaize, Aldi, Lidl, Intermarché, Spar, Carrefour or any other store it shows.

In short

  • You don't create an account, and the app never asks for your name, email, location, contacts or photos.
  • We don't run our own servers and don't collect personal data ourselves.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. In Belgium and the rest of the EEA, Google asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time with the Privacy button on the store list.
  • If the app crashes, Google Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report so we can fix the bug.
  • To show the lists and store logos, the app downloads them from Google Firebase and from Wikimedia Commons, which see your IP address like any website does.
  • We never sell your data.

Legal bases and your consent

Under the GDPR, each use of data rests on one of these bases:

  • Personalised ads and ad measurement: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and the Belgian rules on cookies and device storage). No ad is requested until Google's consent form has been answered.
  • Non-personalised ads: our legitimate interest in funding a free app (Art. 6(1)(f)), within what you allowed in the consent form.
  • Crash reports: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working (Art. 6(1)(f)). Reports carry no name, email or advertising ID.
  • Downloading lists and logos: needed to provide the app you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)).

To change or withdraw your ad consent, tap Privacy at the top of the store list; the consent form opens again. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

How long data is kept, and where

  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days, then deleted.
  • Ad data is kept by Google under its own retention rules, described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Request logs at Google and Wikimedia are kept under their own policies.
  • The cache on your phone is replaced when a list ends and deleted when you uninstall the app.

Google and Wikimedia may process data outside the EEA, including in the United States. Those transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, to which Google is certified, or on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Children

Top 10 Deals is meant for adults doing their grocery shopping and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly process data from children. If you believe a child's data reached us through the services above, contact us and we will help get it deleted.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, a copy to take elsewhere, or a restriction of its use. You can also object to processing based on legitimate interest and withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.

We hold no data that names you, so most requests are best made to Google directly through Google's privacy tools. Write to insomniatft@gmail.com and we will help; we answer within one month.

You can also complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority: Autorité de protection des données / Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit, Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, for example with a new kind of ad or analytics, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released. Questions: insomniatft@gmail.com.
