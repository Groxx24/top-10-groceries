# Store logos

The store logos on the store list (`data/hardcoded/HardcodedCatalog.kt`) are not in the repo:
they are 330 px thumbnails loaded at runtime from Wikimedia Commons. A logo is used only when it
is free of copyright where the app is used (Belgium, under EU law), not just in the US; a store
without one shows its name's first letter instead. Being free of copyright does not make a logo
free to use any way we like: every one is still its owner's trademark. So the logos are only used
to name the store, never changed, never in the app icon or name, and the store list ends with
`stores_disclaimer` ("Not affiliated with or endorsed by the stores shown…"). Checked on
2026-10-14.

A new store's logo must be on Commons itself (files kept on one Wikipedia only are often there
because they are not free outside the US) with a public-domain tag, or used with the store's
written permission.
